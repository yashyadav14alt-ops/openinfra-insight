TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN

A signal is a prompt to review, not an automatic decision.

Scores combine range checks, a simple baseline deviation, and asset criticality. They are intentionally explainable and should be reviewed by a person.

METHOD NOTE

Small, auditable signals

Each synthetic asset is checked against its supplied reference range. A 13-day baseline mean and standard deviation provide a simple context for the latest reading. The risk score also includes the sample asset criticality (1–5).

  • High: outside the reference band or at least 2.4 baseline standard deviations away.
  • Watch: score is at least 52 or deviation reaches 1.7 standard deviations.
  • Normal: neither rule is triggered.

This is a learning prototype, not a validated predictive model. Real deployments need domain review, calibrated sensors, representative data, drift monitoring, access control, and incident procedures. Do not use the sample score for safety or maintenance decisions.
