## Why this project

The most transferable overlap in the ₹75,000 group is the combination of time-series reasoning, anomaly-style signal review, data validation, a small API, searchable information, and a usable dashboard. OpenInfra Insight demonstrates those ideas with **fictional public-utility assets and local notes**. It also shows where its evidence stops: it has no real telemetry, trained model, SQL database, operational GIS, or sensitive-system integration.

This is an independent portfolio sample. It does not reconstruct any listed employer's system, data, procedures, simulation, or operational requirements. It must not be used for real maintenance, security, or safety decisions.

## Eligibility is separate from a portfolio

A project can show skills but cannot replace degree, specialisation, availability, documentation, or other application requirements. Verify your qualifications against each live listing and complete your application profile before applying. The student portal sends your profile as currently saved with an application.
